TurboPanel High Availability is the easy choice: fast worldwide, always on, and ready without another stack to operate. Self-hosting is available for teams with strict infrastructure requirements.

## Source

Five sibling Git repositories (not a monorepo), bootstrapped by [dev](https://github.com/turbopanel/dev). Local checkouts sit under `$HOME`. How they fit together is in [development architecture](/docs/architecture/development-architecture); bugs go to the [issue tracker](https://github.com/turbopanel/turbopanel/issues).
